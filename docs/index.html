The test

Does a good MRT win votes?

For each contested constituency from GE2011 to GE2025, the swing is the change in PAP vote share against what the same homes voted at the previous election, rebuilt block by block when the boundaries moved. The swing was then tested against MRT access (the share of HDB homes within 800 m of a station on polling day), new stations opened since the last election, and exposure to the two biggest breakdowns before an election. Every test also controls for the national swing that year, the previous result, and GRC vs SMC.

All tests

"pts" means percentage points of PAP vote share. p is from regressions with robust (HC1) errors. "Shuffle p" comes from re-running each test 5,000 times with the MRT measure shuffled between constituencies in the same election, which checks the result without relying on textbook assumptions. Nine tests were run, so one or two p-values under 0.05 could appear by luck alone. A result only counts here if it stays under about 0.006 (0.05 ÷ 9).

Data. Boundary shapes are the Elections Department (ELD) "Electoral Boundary" GeoJSON datasets for 2006, 2011, 2015, 2020 and 2025, as published on data.gov.sg. Seat counts per constituency come from Wikipedia's lists of Singaporean electoral divisions, and each year's total was checked against the official count (84 / 87 / 89 / 93 / 97). GE2006 is the earliest year ELD publishes as map data. Older maps (1988–2001) exist only as images, so they are not included.
Method. Every boundary set is clipped to the 2025 coastline, because the older files include territorial sea. This means the earlier maps also show land that was reclaimed later. To find redrawn areas, the map is split into a 100 m grid. A spot keeps its identity only when its old constituency mostly became its new one and the new one was mostly made from the old one. Spots that fail this test are marked redrawn, so a constituency that only changed its name (for example Moulmein-Kallang to Jalan Besar) still counts as redrawn. "Land redrawn" is measured by area, not by number of voters, so large non-residential areas such as Tengah, the catchment and Jurong Island weigh heavily. The "came from" shares on a constituency are also by land area. Shapes are simplified to about 15 m.
Votes. ELD official results by candidate, GE2006–GE2025 (data.gov.sg "Parliamentary General Election Results by Candidate"). National totals are valid votes in contested constituencies only. Walkovers (7 in 2006, 1 in 2011, 1 in 2025) had no vote. The 2006, 2011, 2015, 2020 and 2025 PAP shares this page computes (66.6%, 60.1%, 69.9%, 61.2%, 65.6%) match the official national figures. Results are only published per constituency, so there are no ward-level counts.
MRT test. Homes are HDB residential blocks (HDB Property Information on data.gov.sg, with coordinates), counted from their completion year. Private housing is not included. Station locations come from LTA. Opening dates for each stage come from LTA and sgtrains.com. LRT is excluded. "Within 800 m" means straight-line distance. The breakdown tests count homes whose nearest station was on the NSL/EWL (GE2015, after the 7 Jul 2015 breakdown) or was one of the nine EWL stations from Boon Lay to Queenstown (GE2025, after the 25–30 Sep 2024 disruption). This is a correlation study of about 105 constituency results, so it cannot prove cause. The code is in the repo.
